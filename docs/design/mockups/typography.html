Which type pairing?

Memo needs three voices: a display serif for headlines and chord symbols, a sans for labels and buttons (Inter in all three — it's the boring correct answer), and a mono for measured values. The mono matters more than it looks: monospaced key/BPM readouts are what make the numbers feel measured by DSP rather than written by a chatbot. Click one.
