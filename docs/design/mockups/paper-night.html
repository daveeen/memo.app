One theme, dimmer paper

3 · Newsprint (dimmed paper)

Still one theme, but the paper is aged newsprint rather than white — much softer on a projector and at night, while keeping black-ink contrast. No second theme to maintain.

One theme · softer · less crisp

Cost note, honestly: option 2 is the only one that adds real build time on a 2.5-day clock — roughly a second colour-token set plus a pass to catch contrast bugs. Options 1 and 3 are the same amount of work as each other.
