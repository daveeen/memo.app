3 · Mono + One

Everything is a warm grey step from paper to ink; the single orange-red appears only where audio is live or a chorus is playing. Maximum discipline — colour becomes information.

Most rigorous · sections lean on value, not hue

Note on the structure map: options 1 and 2 distinguish sections by hue; option 3 distinguishes mostly by value (light-to-dark). Value survives a bad projector and colour-blindness better, but hue is faster to scan. Whichever you pick, we'll label every section with text too — never colour alone.
